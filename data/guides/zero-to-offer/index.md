Zero to Offer (Z2O) is Pitt Computer Science Club's flagship career curriculum. Every fall, officers who have interned across Big Tech, quant trading, startups, and local Pittsburgh companies run workshops, resume reviews, and mock interviews to help Pitt students land offers.

These seven guides put that entire playbook in one place.

## Why Intern?

An internship is a 10–12 week full-time trial run as a software engineer between school years. Beyond the pay (**$10K–$42K** for a summer, check real numbers on [Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/internships/)), internships are the single biggest lever for landing a full-time job out of college:

- **Return-offer pipelines:** At many large tech and finance firms, the majority of entry-level hires come from their own intern class.
- **Real engineering reps:** You ship production code, deal with code reviews and legacy systems, and learn things Pitt classes don't cover until upper-level electives.
- **Signal & optionality:** Once you have one legitimate internship on your resume, getting interviews the next cycle gets dramatically easier.

- Work on legitimate projects that challenge you to apply what you learn in school
- Get to meet and hang out with really smart and cool students from all over the world

4. **[4. Resumes & ATS](/guides/zero-to-offer/resume):** Surviving the 7-second skim, Jake's template, `#resume-reviews`, `vmock.com/pitt`, and Google XYZ bullets.
5. **[5. Behavioral Interviews](/guides/zero-to-offer/behavioral-interviews):** Passing the vibe check, linear STAR stories, `💻 + 🏢 + 🚀`, and selling potential without prior internships.
6. **[6. Technical Interviews & OAs](/guides/zero-to-offer/technical-interviews):** Auto vs. non-auto OAs, CodeSignal GCA strategy, the T.A.C.O. framework, NeetCode 150, and `#mock-interview`.
7. **[7. Post-Interview, Offers & Rejection](/guides/zero-to-offer/post-interview):** Rejection autopsies, accelerating competing pipelines, offer-extension emails, and reneging realities.
